Nave Installments Simulator

Simulate financial surcharges and dynamically calculate prices to absorb Nave fees.

Calculate real customer prices by simulating the financial costs of card transactions through Nave. Dynamically increase price list values based on interest, VAT, and fixed fees.

Key Features

Commission Rules

Define rules for direct interest rates, VAT on interest, and additional fixed fees per bank or payment method.

Dynamic Pricelists

Configure pricelists that automatically compute the surcharge, ensuring the merchant receives the exact payout amount after Nave commissions.

Multi-Company Isolation

Allows isolated rule definitions and pricelist simulations per company, matching your global Odoo multi-company environment.

Technical Details

  • Computed Fields: Fully optimized depends-based recalculation of financial costs.
  • Strict Company Scopes: Integrated with `check_company=True` and Multi-Company security rules to prevent data leakage.
